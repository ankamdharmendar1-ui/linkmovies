The Missing 1980s Bollywood Sci-Fi Wave You Never Knew About

Imagine an alternate universe where Bollywood made Star Wars-level space operas in 1985. It almost happened.

Just Now 4 min read By Dharmendar

Whenever we talk about Indian Sci-Fi, everyone immediately brings up Mr. India or skips straight to modern stuff like Kalki 2898 AD. But what if I told you there was a massive, hidden movement in the 1980s where Bollywood producers were desperately trying to make massive space operas and robot movies, and almost all of them got buried?

The Star Wars Hangover

After Star Wars absolutely demolished the global box office, a bunch of ambitious Indian directors tried to jump on the bandwagon. I went down a massive rabbit hole of old film magazines from the 80s, and the concept art I found was wild. We're talking heroes in silver foil suits, heavily armed robot villains, and spaceships made out of modified ambassador car parts.

Why Did They Disappear?

So, where did these movies go? Two words: Budget and Fear. Creating practical sci-fi effects is insanely expensive. A lot of these movies shot a few reels of footage, realized the spaceships looked like cheap plastic toys on camera, and quietly shut down production. The studios were terrified of being laughed at by audiences who had just seen Hollywood's state-of-the-art VFX.

The Leftovers

A few movies did survive, usually by pivoting to comedy or relying heavily on magic rather than science. But somewhere out there, in a dusty, forgotten film vault in Mumbai, there are reels of completely wild, unreleased 80s Indian sci-fi movies just waiting to be discovered. It's a crazy piece of lost cinema history that deserves to be seen.
